07 Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

If something isn't covered here, add a note to your booking request and we'll follow up.
What's included in a clean?

Standard visits cover kitchens, bathrooms, living areas and bedrooms — dusting, surfaces, floors and fixtures. Deep cleans add baseboards, appliance interiors and detail work. Exact scope is confirmed with your request.

How is a quote calculated?

Pricing depends on home size, service type and condition. We don't publish flat rates online — send your details through the booking request and we'll follow up with a quote before confirming your visit.

How do I request a date?

Use the calendar in the hero or the booking form further down the page. Selecting a date submits it as a preference — it becomes a confirmed appointment once we've reviewed and responded.

Do I need to be home?

It's your choice. Some clients are home during the visit, others arrange access in advance. Let us know your preference in the notes field when you book.

Can I send photos?

Yes — the booking form includes an optional photo upload for areas you'd like us to pay particular attention to.
